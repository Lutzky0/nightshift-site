Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

What Nightshift is

Nightshift is a personal tool operated by a single individual to schedule and publish their own content to accounts they own on platforms such as YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When an account is connected, Nightshift receives only the data the platform grants through its official API, such as access tokens, basic profile information (account ID, username), the content being published, and performance statistics (such as views, likes and comments) for content published to the owner's accounts.

How it is used

This data is used solely to authenticate with the platform, publish content on the account owner's behalf, and show the owner how their own posts are performing. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Storage and security

Tokens and content are stored only on infrastructure controlled by the operator and are protected with reasonable security measures. Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the service.

Deleting your data

You can revoke Nightshift's access at any time from the connected platform's app settings. To request deletion of any stored data, email nightshift289@gmail.com; it will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

Nightshift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

This policy may be updated; changes take effect when posted on this page.
